 1. OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 `scripts/genbank_meor.py` is a specialized, domain-specific bioinformatic scanner 
 and BGC/operon mining engine designed to evaluate isolate genomes (.gbff, .gbk) 
 for MEOR capabilities, hydrocarbon catabolism (alkanes, aromatics, anaerobic 
 activation), and biosurfactant/bio-emulsifier biosynthesis potential.

 The tool operates computationally via Biopython (Bio.SeqIO) without loading full 
 flat files into the AI context window, outputting structured diagnostic reports.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. DATABASE INTEGRATION (CANT-HYD + HMDB + HADEG)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Marker definitions, EC numbers, KEGG Orthology (KO) IDs, gene symbols, and product
 regex patterns were synthesized from three curated bioinformatic databases:

  1. CANT-HYD (Calgary approach to ANnoTating HYDrocarbon degradation genes):
     - Aerobic & anaerobic aliphatic + aromatic degradation markers.
     - Includes 37 marker HMM targets: alkB, almA, ladA, bmoA, CYP153, prmA,
       ndoA, dmpN, tmoA, assA/masD (anaerobic alkane), bssA (anaerobic toluene), 
       nmsA (anaerobic methylnaphthalene).

  2. HMDB (Hydrocarbon Monooxygenase Gene Database):
     - Hydrocarbon hydroxylation monooxygenases (38 genes across 11 enzyme classes).
     - Markers included: pmoA/mmoX (methane), alkB (alkanes), amoA (alkene/ammonia), 
       smoA (styrene).

  3. HADEG (Hydrocarbon Aerobic Degradation Enzymes & Genes Database):
     - Experimentally validated hydrocarbon, plastic, and biosurfactant markers.
     - Rhamnolipids: rhlA (HAA synthase), rhlB (mono-rhamnolipid), rhlC (di-rhamnolipid),
       rhlR/rhlI (quorum sensing).
     - Lipopeptides (NRPS): srfA-A/B/C/D (surfactin), licA-D (lichenysin), 
       fenA-E/ppsA-E (fengycin/plipastatin), ituA-C (iturin), vsnA-C (viscosin).
     - Trehalolipids & Glycolipids: sdtA, tshA, otsA/otsB.
     - Polymeric Bio-emulsifiers: wza/wzb/wzc (emulsan/alasan exopolysaccharides).

 4. ALGORITHMIC & SYSTEM DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Feature Filtering: Scans CDS and misc_feature elements to avoid duplicate hits 
    from bare gene feature records.
  - Multi-Tier Weighted Scoring:
      * Weight 3 (High Confidence): Exact KEGG KO, EC number, or gene symbol match.
      * Weight 2 (Medium Confidence): Specific product keyword match.
      * Weight 1 (Low Confidence): Broad product/note keyword match.
  - Spatial Operon & BGC Cluster Discovery:
      * Partitioned strictly by contig to prevent cross-contig clustering.
      * Groups co-localized hits with intergenic distance <= 200 bp (configurable via --max-gap).
  - Pathway Completeness Profile:
      * Evaluates percentage of required enzymatic steps present in genome for 5 key MEOR pathways.
  - Genomic Density Karyograms:
      * ASCII visual density overview mapping MEOR hits per contig in 50 kb windows.
  - Multi-Format CLI Output:
      * Human-readable text console report (default).
      * Programmatic JSON format (--format json).
      * Tab-delimited TSV presence/absence matrix (--format tsv).
